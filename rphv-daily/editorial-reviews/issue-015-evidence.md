## Checked factual assertions
- Mahindra confirmed on **6 November 2026** 5-star Bharat NCAP rating for the **factory-fitted CNG XUV 3XO ZNG**. AOP **29.36/32**, COP **45/49** under qualifying conditions.
- Bharat NCAP live vehicle listing notes **CNG: November 2026 onwards**, updated rating and an explicit production-cutoff note: **from VIN MA1NS2NVPS2B98575 onwards COP 45/49**, **before cutoff COP 43/49**.
- The revised COP vehicle-assessment portion is **9/13**, compared with **7/13** for earlier vehicles, while dynamic and CRS fitment portions are **24/24** and **12/12**; the total is **45/49** for qualifying production.
- Bharat NCAP notes the CNG AX5 underwent a frontal offset crash test; side and pole results were carried over based on the unchanged body structure and safety systems. Do not claim independent full crash testing for every variant.
- These scores are **technical standardized test measurements**, not production, sales, registrations, injuries avoided or guaranteed real-world safety outcomes.

## Editorial interpretation (not independently measured results)
Do not communicate a generic badge without checking VIN and exact rated variant; verify owner-facing safety equipment, safety-critical parts fitment and paperwork. All five suggested departmental KPIs are proposals by RPHV, not OEM or Bharat NCAP benchmarks.
