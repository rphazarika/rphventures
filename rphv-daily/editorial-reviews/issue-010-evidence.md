### Reported data and definitions
- **Passenger-vehicle registrations**: 1,82,728, Navratri 2026 (11–20 October; ten days).
- **PV registrations 2024**: 1,65,534, Navratri 2024 (3–12 October; ten days). FADA's comparison: **+10.39%**.
- **PV registrations 2025**: 2,34,004, Navratri 2025 (22 September–2 October; **eleven days**). FADA comparison: **−21.91%**, not a like-for-like festival-duration comparison.
- **Overall vehicle retail 2026**: 11,23,086 registrations. Not used as the PV figure.
- Compiled as at **21 October 2026** from **1,468 of 1,469** RTOs, in collaboration with MoRTH; preliminary, subject to later revisions.
- FADA states Telangana registrations for three-wheelers, commercial vehicles and tractors were not available. PV is not one of the excluded categories.
- **These are customer retail registrations, not manufacturer-to-dealer wholesales.** No 2026 full-month October retail number is claimed.
- Release contextualizes unusual 2025 base with an additional festival day and 2025 GST 2.0 rollout. This is FADA's explanatory view, not proof of causation for an individual dealer.
