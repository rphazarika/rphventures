EDITORIAL GOVERNANCE

Facts first.
Every day.

RPHV Daily is an AI-assisted publication with an evidence-led editorial process. Automated drafts are not automatically verified facts.

01

Original sources

We prefer SIAM, FADA, VAHAN/MoRTH, RBI, government notifications, stock-exchange filings and official OEM communications. A secondary report is labelled as such and never silently substituted for an unavailable original.

02

Meaning of numbers

Every number must show its reporting period, date of source publication, unit, geography and measurement basis. Manufacturer wholesales must never be represented as customer registrations, and estimates must not be represented as audited facts.

03

Review and approval

Source-backed drafts undergo consistency checks. Unsupported or disputed claims are held back. While the portal remains in preview, no edition is represented as a fully approved public publication.

04

Corrections

We will attach correction notes with what changed, when and why, while preserving the original reporting period. Substantive corrections should never be made invisibly.

05

Independence

Practical process recommendations are editorial opinions, not official OEM instructions. Regulatory advice should cite primary law or rules and be checked against current requirements before application.

06

AI transparency

AI may help research, summarise, draft and format. Source documents, not AI-generated text, are the evidence for factual claims. A missing source means the claim remains unverified.

ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

Ideas grounded
in operations.

RPHV Daily brings automotive market intelligence closer to the decisions made by dealership owners, sales teams, service managers, parts teams, used-car operations and accounts departments.

Our editorial objective is not to repeat the day's news. It is to identify what a credible development means, what teams should check and which metric deserves attention.
